01 — About

The story behind the numbers.

I'm a Data & Business Analyst based in Bengaluru (open to Mumbai), currently leading HR Analytics & Business Intelligence at Shadowfax Technologies. My work sits at the intersection of operations, finance, and people — where workforce decisions translate directly into cost, productivity, and retention.

Day to day I drive a ₹6Cr+ monthly P&L, run PMS for 1500+ employees, design target-based, holiday and peak-season incentives, and lead an 8-member analytics team. I came to analytics from Mechanical Engineering, picked up SQL and BI tools through an IIT Kanpur–affiliated certification, and have since built the kind of dashboards leadership actually opens at 9 AM.

Lately I've been blurring the line between analyst and product builder — authoring BRDs, prototyping an in-house HRMS on Supabase + Railway, and shipping with vibe coding using Claude Code & Cursor. My favorite kind of project? One where the dashboard doesn't just report a number — it changes one.
